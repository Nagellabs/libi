## What you can do

GPL-3.0 is an [OSI-approved](https://opensource.org/licenses/GPL-3.0) open source license.
You can:

- **Use libi for anything**, personal or commercial, with no seat cap, signup, or time limit.
- **Use it commercially** — make videos for paying clients, run it across your company,
  build internal tooling on it.
- **Modify it** however you like, and run your modified version privately forever. Private
  use and internal modification trigger no obligations at all.
- **Fork it, publish your fork, and even sell it.** Selling GPL software is explicitly
  permitted.
- **Sell services built on it** — agency work, consulting, production, training.

## What GPL asks in return

If you **distribute** libi — original or modified, as source or as a binary — you must:

- License what you distribute under GPL-3.0, and
- Make the corresponding source available to whoever you distributed it to.

That's the deal: improvements that get shipped to others stay open. Nothing is owed for
anything you keep to yourself.

The output you create with libi — your videos — is **yours**. The license covers libi's
code, not what you make with it.

## What libi sells

The editor is free, forever. Revenue comes from **one optional paid membership**, for a
single user or a team. It is never required, and it never gates anything already in the box.

**Included:**

- **Team sharing** — pieces, assets, characters, and items shared across teammates.
- **Cloud backup** — your work backed up and restorable from another machine.
- **In app AI coding and generation model (opt-in)** — use libi's hosted models for the
  coding chat and for generation instead of your own API keys, with usage tracking and
  per-member limits.

**Planned to join the membership later:**

- **Viral DB** — a continuously-updated database of trending videos with structured
  analyses your agent can search.
- **Posting scheduler & analytics** — auto-publish to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X, and
  Reddit on a schedule, then feed engagement data back to the agent.
- More over time. The membership grows; the editor stays free.

These are **cloud services**. They run on libi's servers, and their code is not part of this
repository and is not covered by the GPL — the same open-core split used by GitLab, Grafana,
and many others. The client code that talks to them is open source like everything else here.

## Trademark

The GPL covers copyright, not trademark. The **libi** name and logo are not licensed under
it. You're welcome to fork the code; please give your fork its own name so users can tell
your build apart from ours.

## Why GPL

We went open source rather than "source-available" because we'd rather the project be
genuinely usable, forkable, and auditable than defensively fenced off. That's a deliberate
trade: GPL does **not** stop a competitor from forking libi and selling it. What we rely on
instead is the cloud services, the brand, and simply doing the work well.
