# Ad formats

The creative axis. Pick the format, then read that format's section in [model-seedance-2-formulas](model-seedance-2-formulas.md). Where the clips come from is the footage axis, in [production-routes](production-routes.md).

| Format | Use when | Formulas section |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UGC selfie | an authentic testimonial or reaction, a real person on a phone | UGC selfie |
| Talking-head testimonial | a founder or explainer, direct to camera | UGC selfie, talking-head |
| Product hero | no person, a product beauty shot with elemental interaction | Product hero |
| Feature walkthrough | demo several features, show don't tell | Feature walkthrough |
| Premium reveal | luxury or minimal, black void, text narrative, no dialogue | Premium reveal |
| Studio lookbook | a styled model, several looks, voiceover | Studio lookbook |

## Beats

Every UGC ad is a sequence of purpose-driven beats, not a slideshow of pretty shots. The spine is Hook, Show, Demo, Result, Verdict, and each beat is a jump cut inside the one clip.

| Beat | Purpose | Framing | Example action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | grab attention in 3 s or less | looking into camera | an expressive opener, holds the product up |
| Show | product detail | closer to the lens | tilts or turns the product, shows label or texture |
| Demo | proof of use | extreme close-up | applies or uses it, shows it working |
| Result | evidence | mirror or a different angle | points at the outcome, before and after |
| Verdict | final opinion and CTA | back to the opening angle | holds the product up, delivers the CTA line |

Count by duration: about 10 s takes two or three beats (Hook, one demonstration, Verdict); 15 s takes three or four. Match the dialogue to the beats with the word-count method in [craft](../references/craft.md); do not cram five spoken beats into ten seconds.

**At least one beat is a silent action** (a sip, an inspection, a turn of the product, a reaction, reading the label). It feels more authentic, costs no spoken words and stops you cramming dialogue.

Proven variants as starters: talking head plus product (hook with the product in hand, one feature, a verdict, the CTA); unboxing (sealed package, hands enter, the reveal, product in use); before and after (a wide shot of the problem, apply, a time cut, the result mirroring the hook's composition); POV demo (first person, hands enter with the product, the feature in action, a wider shot, the result); voice-driven b-roll (a striking environment, the product appears, a subject enters, the payoff, narration kept separate from any lip-sync).

## Self-check

Read the beat list back: does it read as a real ad (hook, story, payoff, CTA) or a slideshow of disconnected test shots? Rewrite if the latter. A pretty shot that does not advance the sell is dead weight.
