# Platform Specs (UGC video)

Pull from this file at Stage 0 of the skill. Source: platform docs as of 2026-05.

## TikTok

- **Aspect:** 9:16
- **Resolution:** 1080×1920
- **Length:** 9–60s for ads; up to 10 min for organic. UGC sweet spot 15–30s.
- **Hook:** ≤ 3s. First frame must do work.
- **Safe zone:** keep critical text/captions away from the bottom 18% (UI overlap with username/CTA) and top 8%.
- **Sound:** assume sound-on. Captions still required (~80% of viewers leave them on).

## Instagram Reels

- **Aspect:** 9:16
- **Resolution:** 1080×1920
- **Length:** 15–90s. UGC sweet spot 15–30s.
- **Hook:** ≤ 3s.
- **Safe zone:** bottom 20% covered by caption + UI; top ~10% by username.
- **Captions:** auto-generated by IG, but author your own for style + placement control.

## YouTube Shorts

- **Aspect:** 9:16
- **Resolution:** 1080×1920
- **Length:** ≤ 60s.
- **Hook:** ≤ 3s.
- **End card:** YouTube does not show end screens on Shorts; bake CTA into the final beat.

## Square (Meta in-feed, LinkedIn)

- **Aspect:** 1:1
- **Resolution:** 1080×1080
- **Length:** 5–30s sweet spot for paid; up to 60s organic.

## Landscape ad (YouTube pre-roll, web display)

- **Aspect:** 16:9
- **Resolution:** 1920×1080
- **Length:** 6s (bumper), 15s, 30s common ad slots.
- **Hook:** ≤ 5s before the skip button on YouTube.

## Universal advice

- **Captions are required**, regardless of platform. Generate them as text overlays via `libi.add_overlay({ kind: "text" })` so they're crisp and styled — don't rely on the platform's auto-captions.
- **Loudness:** target -14 LUFS (matches Spotify / TikTok normalization).
- **First frame:** treat it like a thumbnail. It's the only frame guaranteed to be seen.
