# Copywriting angles — five different creative bets

A good ad isn't one idea polished — it's several genuinely different *bets* on
what will land. Use this after the brief ([brief-intake.md](brief-intake.md)) to
generate distinct hook + script directions, then take one into
[script-craft.md](script-craft.md).

## The 5-body framework

Write one angle per row. The five should be different *bets*, not edits of each
other. Each gets a one-paragraph creative direction + a UGC example.

1. **Reveal / Story** — first person, present tense, "here's what just happened."
   Walk the viewer through the concrete sequence of events. Highest-context
   variant; let it run a little longer.
   *UGC example:* "Okay so I almost returned this. Two weeks in, my skin's
   calmer than it's been in a year — I have to show you."

2. **Math / Before-after** — quantify the old way vs the new way. Numbers do the
   persuading, not adjectives. Old state → new state, with a real figure.
   *UGC example:* "I was spending forty minutes on my routine. Now it's two
   products and four minutes. Same skin. Look."

3. **Fresh hook** — an angle nobody else is running. Lead with a pattern-breaking
   line that reframes the category.
   *UGC example:* "This isn't a skincare video. It's me apologizing to my old
   dermatologist."

4. **Ultra-short CTA** — tight and punchy. One claim, maybe a couple of fast
   beats, one call to action. Wins on cold traffic.
   *UGC example:* "Redness gone in two weeks. Link's in my bio. That's it."

5. **Authority / Community** — lead with credibility (results, a count, a stat)
   and what the audience joins or gets.
   *UGC example:* "Two thousand people switched to this last month. I finally
   get why."

### Swap-in angles

Use these instead of one of the five when they fit the offer better:

- **Objection-handler** — name the doubt out loud, then dismantle it. *"You're
  thinking it's another overhyped serum. So was I."*
- **Mechanism reveal** — show *how* it works / what's inside, not just the result.

## Rules

- **Specificity beats adjectives.** Quantify. "$150M managed", "47 redos gone",
  "14 days" — concrete numbers, never "amazing results".
- **Model on winners.** When you have access to top-performing ads in the
  category, read them first. Identify *why* each works — the hook pattern, the
  proof point, the CTA style — and carry that DNA into the new variants. Mirror
  the *why*, never copy the words.
- **No forbidden words** in any angle — see
  [forbidden-words.md](forbidden-words.md).

## How to use this in the flow

Produce **2–3 hook variants from different angles** (e.g. one Reveal, one
Math, one Ultra-short CTA). Present them to the user for A/B selection. The user
picks one to generate now; **save the others as a piece note** (the storyboard
**overview** or an `aiGeneration`/notes line) so they're ready for a second variant
without re-deriving the brief.
